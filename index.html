AGE SEGMENTATION

Tailored for ages 8 to 17

We group students carefully by both age group and Spanish level to ensure comfortable, age-appropriate interactions.

8–10 years

Explorers

Visual and playful learning centered on interactive stories, vibrant characters, high-frequency vocabulary, and cheerful introductory dialogues.

11–13 years

Adventurers

Mission-driven challenges, real-world communication scenarios, group problem-solving, and exciting cultural discovery across Hispanic nations.

14–17 years

Teens

Authentic communication tailored to youth interests, travel, music, global topics, debate, and progressive independence in spoken Spanish.
